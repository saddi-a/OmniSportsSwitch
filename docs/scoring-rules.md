# Scoring rules reference

How the scoring, game-state and score-reading rules work and why the constants are what they are (moved from PLAN.md on 2026-10-18; keep it up to date when a rule changes). Numbers live in `settings.toml`; users read README "How a swap is decided".

*1. The rules as they are now*
* **State per tile** (`GameTracker`): live / break / over. Order: over caption (`FINAL`, `FINAL/OT`, `MATCH TERMINÉ`... whole item, 2 readings, kept 120 s) unless a live scorebug (down and distance `3rd & 4`/`2nd Down`, or the quarter `Ath`/`4th`/`Q4`) was seen in the last 15 s (a results ticker shows `Final` for other games) > ad placeholder phrase (also a clock with no scoreboard evidence beside it = ad countdown, 2 readings) > break caption (`HALFTIME`, `MI-TEMPS`) > readable clock frozen longer than the sport allows > overtime banner (no clock in OT, 30 s) > down/quarter seen in the last 20 s (the clock colon is often lost: `14848`) > no clock for `no_clock_s` = break. Bilingual phrase lists are in `settings.toml` `[banners]`.
* **Ad / not live** (`Scorer.components`): picture ad (`on_ad`) = -0.5; sound ad = music >= 0.5, no crowd, music above speech by 0.15, on 3 of the last 5 readings, remembered 12 s = -0.25. An ad is never promoted. `recent_ad_s`: an idle MAIN is not handed to a stream that was on an ad in the last 30 s, unless MAIN shows a picture ad/break/over for certain.
* **Swap rule**: challenger must beat MAIN by 40% and 0.15, score >= 0.35, keep qualifying 2 s (`confirm_s`), 20 s warm-up and 20 s dwell. MAIN idle (ad, break, over) = any live non-ad stream takes over (also during warm-up if the picture says ad/break/over). Make Main hold and Freeze: see the other paragraphs.
* **Score reader** (`ScoreWatcher.update`): skip stat graphics/tickers (`STAT_GRAPHIC_RE`) -> `find_score_geo` on OCR boxes (+ the digit fallback of `screen_ocr.py` when it finds nothing) -> else `find_score` anchored to team names only (loose readings only without boxes) -> vote 3 of the last 5 per side -> event when a side rises 1..10 above the *peak* -> peak only rises after the score held 25 s -> same score not announced twice in 60 s -> events ignored while the tile is break/over. A clock jump resets the baseline.
* **Leverage** (`leverage.py`): -1 (late blowout) .. +1 (late close game) from the voted score, the quarter vote (`GameTracker.period`, OT = 5) or the soccer minute; added to the stream's score as `leverage_weight` x value. Alone it never reaches `min_score`; unknown = 0.
* **Replays** (`GameTracker.replay`, banners in `[banners] replay_words`, EN/FR/ES/PT, 2 readings, kept 6 s): with a scorebug (clock/down/quarter seen in the last 6 s) it is a replay inside the live game: `Scorer.set_replay` multiplies crowd and surge by `replay_dampen` (0.3) and `ScoreWatcher.update(..., replay=True)` ignores the reading. Without a scorebug (studio show, replay package) the state is `break`. The server must call `set_replay(tab, any tile `tracker.replay`)` and pass `replay=tracker.replay` to the watcher (main session).
* **Event size by points** (`ScoreWatcher._event_size`, American football only; other or unknown sports stay full): gain 4+ = touchdown = 1.0; 2-3 (safety, field goal) and a lone 1 = `field_goal_strength` (0.6); a 1 or 2 within `extra_point_s` (30 s) of that side's touchdown = extra point / conversion = no event. `ScoreWatcher(config)` and `update(..., sport=...)`; the size is in `watcher.strength` after an event (the server passes it to `Scorer.set_event(tab, now, strength)`).
* **Shouting** (`weights.shout`, default 0 = off): `Scorer.set_tags(..., shout=)` with the strongest of `SHOUT_CLASSES` (Shout, Yell, Screaming); ignored when music >= `idle_music`. Checked on the logs of 2026-10-10/11 (top-3 classes only, 18303 lines): Yell/Screaming/Shout reach the top 3 only 14 times, 9 of them in ads (Yell up to 0.15 under music), so it ships at 0 until the server logs `shout=` on live goals; then try 0.2.
* **Scorebug hidden (measuring only)**: DAZN shows no REPLAY text; it hides the scorebug for 7-25 s (seen 2026-10-11, COM-ROM: 8 gaps in 30 min, game clock kept running). `GameTracker.scorebug_note` is `("hidden", s)` when a scorebug seen within `scorebug_memory_s` (60) has been missing `scorebug_hidden_s` (6), then `("back", s)` on return or `("cancelled", s)` when an ad/break/over ended the gap (ad and break gaps are never reported). `GameTracker(sport, config)`. It changes no score or state; the server only logs it to compare with real goals before deciding to use it.
* **Anticipation cues (measuring only)**: banners/graphics before a likely score. `GameTracker.anticipation` lists the cues reported on a reading (2 readings in a row, per-cue cooldown `anticipation_cooldown_s` 30 s, never during ad/break/over/replay). Phrase cues come from `[banners] anticipation_words` (EN/FR/ES/PT; soccer, American football; red card / penalty kick stay big plays); structural cues: `4th down` (also OCR `Ath & 3`), `goal to go` (`1st & GOAL`), `big gain` (`+NN YDS`, >= `anticipation_big_gain_yd` 20), `yard line` (`N YD` <= `anticipation_goal_yd` 10 beside a down text), `added time` (`+N` beside a clock at 45-50 or 90-100). `Scorer.set_anticipation(tab, now)` + `weights.anticipation` (0 = off; fades over `anticipation_s` 18 s; try 0.15, it must stay under `min_score` alone). Replayed on the logs of 2026-10-10/11: 4th down 48, goal to go 36, flag 25, review 3 (RULING ON FIELD), red zone 2, field goal attempt 1, added time 3 (COM-ROM 45+1). Not yet compared with real goals.
* **Replay by clock (measure first)**: DAZN replays keep a scorebug whose clock is the replayed moment's. `GameTracker.clock_replay_note` is `("start", s back)` when the game clock goes back `replay_clock_min_s`..`replay_clock_max_s` (10-30 s, against the direction it runs in, learned from the first steps) and either runs on from there (next reading) or, after a scorebug gap of 6 s or more, the live clock returns (single odd reading); `("end", s)` when the live clock jumps forward again. `replay_by_clock_s` = 0 (default) only logs; above 0 also sets `tracker.replay` for that long (dampening + score reader). Limits: a 10-30 s seek by the viewer looks the same; rewinds of minutes do not count. Replayed on the logs: Como-Roma 13:59:32 (the red-card replay, 20 s back) and 13:41:26 (25 s back, probably the user's own seek).
* **Lone clock vs a graphic over the scoreboard** (`GameTracker._continues_game_clock`): a clock with no scoreboard text beside it is an ad countdown only if it does NOT continue the clock last read beside a scoreboard (moved by the elapsed time either way, or not at all, within 3 s) in the last 15 s. Found live: a `NO MORE SUBS AVAILABLE` banner hid the team codes for two readings and caused a false 8 s `break` at 89:07 (Como-Roma 14:23:40). A real ad countdown starts at an unrelated value, or loses the grace after 15 s.
* **Bare `FT` beside a running clock** (found live, Como-Roma 14:28:57): a MATCH MOMENTUM graphic (`45'`, `FT`) made the state `over` for 2 minutes at 94:26. A bare `FT` item now counts as the over caption only when the game clock is not running (changed in the last 3 s); `FULL TIME`, `FINAL` etc. are unchanged.
* **Name-before-digits scorebug** (found live, Eagles-Jaguars 17:44:32, false touchdown): `PHI| 14  3-1  JAX 7  3RD 9:39  37` was read (7, 37) (JAX's 7 and the play clock 37) because the item `PHI| 14` was not split and `JAX` sits between the scores; the real 14 then fired a +7 event that promoted a stream. `find_score_geo` now splits `CODE| digits` (`CODE_SCORE_RE`) and `name_left`: when each number has its own team name right before it, that pair is allowed (no outside-the-names rule, no label-size rule) and wins over pairs from elsewhere on the row. Fixture: `EAGLES` in `test_samples_1011.py`.
* **Stray clocks no longer restart the no-clock timer** (found live, Eagles-Jaguars 17:51:30): a GAME LEADERS table gave the clock `80:40`; every clock reading restarted the 90 s no-clock timer, so a break filled with graphics stayed `live`. In `GameTracker.update` a clock is dropped (for the timer, the freeze check and the scorebug memory; ad detection still sees it) when it is above `MAX_CLOCK_MIN` for the sport (American football 20 min, others 130), or when a stat-graphic phrase (`STAT_GRAPHIC_RE`) is on screen with no scorebug cue (down/quarter, clock+code, two team codes). `no_clock_s` itself is unchanged (the user decides; 90 s for American football).
* **Scorebug with an unreadable clock** (found live, Liverpool-Man City 17:54:48, false 3 s break and a swap): the compact scorebug stayed up but its clock was read on 1 frame in 5 (`2109`, `21-20`, `22*45`, `23810`: the colon is read as another character). With two team codes read in the last `CODES_MEMORY_S` (10 s) the no-clock limit is doubled (soccer 90 s, American football 180 s); without them it stays `no_clock_s`. Reader idea for `screen_ocr.find_clocks` (main session): accept `\d\d[-*8 ]\d\d` / 4 digits beside team codes as a clock.
* **Late field goal** (found live, Eagles-Jaguars): the real 7 -> 10 (JAX field goal) was announced at 17:56:59 although `JAX] 10` was on screen from about 17:53:41, because the separator after the code is also read as `/` and `]` (`PHI/ 14`, `JAX] 10`). `CODE_SCORE_RE` accepts `| ! I l / ] [`; replayed, the event now fires at 17:53:41.
* **Full-screen score card** (decided by the user 2026-10-11, found live: the field goal at 17:50:40 showed `WEEK 5` / `14 10` / `3RD QTR` while the scorebug was hidden and was announced 3 min late): `find_score_card` in `score_watch.py`, used by `ScoreWatcher.update` only when the scorebug readers find nothing. It needs a score voted before (`_stable`), ONE two-number item at least `CARD_VS_LABEL` (2.0) x as tall as the quarter text (`3RD QTR`, `CARD_QUARTER_RE`), `WEEK n` or a clock on screen, and both numbers not lower than before and at most `MAX_RISE` above (so the order is the scorebug's). Then the normal vote and event rules apply (replayed: the field goal fires at 17:50:44). Not cards: a stats table, a results ticker (no quarter), a record (`2-2`). The separator after a team code also accepts `}{)(`.
* **Learning the sport earlier (stage A of 3, branch `scoring-bugs-34`; the user chose all four sources 2026-10-11).** Priority, a higher source always replaces a lower one: 1 popup choice, 2 hard scorebug cue (`SportDetector.sport`, as before), 3 feed league when team names match (stage B, needs the accessor from `feed_map.py`), 4 tab title league words from the extension (stage C), 5 league words on screen (`SportDetector.weak_sport`: `LEAGUE_WORDS`, whole words, +1 per reading, needs `WEAK_MIN` 3 = about 4 readings and 1.5x the runner-up), 6 the remembered sport per tab (`daemon/sport_memory.py`, `SportMemory(path)`, `get(tab_id, now)`, `remember(tab_id, sport, now)`, `save()`; wall-clock time, forgotten after 12 h; a start guess only). Replayed on the live logs after the 18:00 restart: the league words alone gave the NFL tab its sport at 18:01:41 (hard cue 18:03:04), so the memory (first reading) matters most. Not wired into `server.py` yet (main session): see the stage message.
* **Cue weights by subject (branch `scoring-bugs-35`, decided by the user 2026-10-11, option C).** `daemon/cues.py` (pure): `group_of(cue, sport)` puts every cue in a group, `banner_tier(text, sport)` reads the subject from a banner (`VAR: POSSIBLE GOAL`, `REVIEW: TOUCHDOWN`, `PENALTY CHECK`; in American football a penalty is a flag = mid), `SpeechMemory(window_s).record(tab, cues, text, sport, now)` / `.tier(tab, now)` keeps the subjects the commentary named in the last 20 s (touchdown/goal/penalty kick/red card = top, interception/fumble = high, offside/foul/yellow card/flag = mid; the highest wins; `card` is ignored in American football because `troisieme carton` = third quarter misheard), `cue_weight(cue, banner, speech_tier, sport, weights)`. Review cues (`review`, `under review`, `replay review`, `challenge`, `var`, `var check`, `revision`, `contestation`, `assistance video`): subject on the banner wins over the commentary, no subject = low. Weights (`Weights`, `settings.toml`, popup): `anticipation` = master scale (now 1.0; 0 = off), `cue_review_top` 0.50 / `high` 0.35 / `mid` 0.20 / `low` 0.10, `cue_chance` 0.25 (red zone, goal to go, yard line, field goal (attempt), free throws, buzzer beater, penalty shootout, penalty kick in soccer), `cue_minor` 0.10 (corner, free kick, flag, 4th down, big gain, added time, timeout, penalty in American football). `Scorer.set_anticipation(tab, now, value)`: the strongest fading cue stays; the cue is multiplied by 1 + `cue_leverage_gain` (0.5) x leverage (x0.5..x1.5) and by the master scale; with no other excitement (crowd + surge + shout + event below `cue_support` 0.10) it adds at most `cue_alone_cap` 0.30 and never more than min_score - 0.01 minus the leverage bonus, so a cue alone cannot take MAIN. `GameTracker` now also reports review banners that name a subject (`REVIEW_BANNER_RE`) and `anticipation_text` (cue -> banner text); a review of a big play (`REVIEW: INTERCEPTION`, `VAR: RED CARD`) is no longer a big-play event. Server wiring is the main session's.
* **Tests never touch the real `logs/`**: `daemon/tests/conftest.py` points `daemon.server.LOG_DIR` at the test's tmp folder (sports.json, feed recordings); `Server(sport_memory_path=...)` takes the memory file from the caller. Test: the real `sports.json` is unchanged after a server run.
* **Sport precedence (stage B, branch `scoring-bugs-37`)**: `daemon/sport_choice.py` `choose_sport(popup, hard, feed, title, weak, memory) -> (sport, source)`; the order of `SOURCES` is the user's (popup > hard scorebug cue > feed league > tab title > league words on screen > remembered sport), a higher source always replaces a lower one, empty or unknown names are ignored. `sport_from_feed(feed.LEAGUES[league][0])` maps the feed's sport names (`soccer`, `american football`) to ours. The feed league comes from `FeedMapper.mapping(tab, tile).league`.
* **Tab title (stage C, branch `scoring-bugs-38`)**: `sport_detect.sport_from_title(title)`: the same whole league words as on the screen (`LEAGUE_WORDS`), None when no league or two sports are named. The extension sends the title as `title` on the register message; the server stores `tab_titles[tab_id]` and passes it to `choose_sport(title=...)`.
* **Player list read as a score** (found live by the feed gate, Liverpool-Man City 18:16:20, false `score change` at 0-0): a `TOP 3` list (`Adverse`, `BARCOLA 5`, `Cherki 2` in a column) was read by the text-only anchored reader as `TEAM n TEAM n` = (5, 2). Two fixes: `STAT_GRAPHIC_RE` also knows `TOP n`, `statistics`, `statistiques`, `ballons`; and `_numbers_read_as_a_score` (used when the geometry reader found nothing and the text reader's two numbers are separate items) accepts them only when they are on one row or two neighbours of one column (no third number between), so a column of stats is never a score. Idea for the server (main session): a score change that the feed contradicts for a mapped game is suspicious.
* **End-zone cue graded by distance (American football, branch `scoring-bugs-40`, option 2 of 3; option 3 = also from the feed, API session)**: `cues.end_zone_factor(yards, down, goal_to_go, cfg) -> 0..1 | None` (1.0 at `cue_zone_near_yd` 3 yards or closer and for a 4th down within 3 yards of the goal line, 0.0 at `cue_zone_far_yd` 10 or more, linear; None = unknown), `cues.zone_weight(cue, factor, weights)` (`goal to go`, `yard line`, `red zone`: `cue_zone_far` 0.15 .. `cue_zone_near` 0.40; no distance = today's `cue_chance` 0.25), `cue_weight(..., zone=factor)`. `GameTracker` reads `down`, `goal_to_go` and `end_zone_yards` (a yard line graphic `5 YD` beside a down text, at most 25 yards; never in a stats graphic: `STAT_GRAPHIC_RE` or a stat line like `7 REC, 68 YDS`; never in other sports). The real scorebug shows `1st & GOAL` with NO distance (18:37:55), so today the OCR mostly falls back to the plain cue; the feed is the good source of the yard line. A cue alone is still capped (test on the strongest value).
* **Shots-per-period table read as a score** (found live, Liverpool-Man City 18:41:34, false `score change` (5, 3) at 0-0 and a wrong swap): the text-only reader (`find_score` rule 2, `TEAM n (noise) TEAM n`) took `MCI ... 5 Periode 3`: `Periode` counted as a team name and nothing looked at positions; the lone `0` beside LIV only gave the earlier (0, 5) readings. Fixes: `NOT_A_TEAM` also holds `periode`, `periode`, `tirs`, `tir` and rule 2 now honours it; `STAT_GRAPHIC_RE` knows `tirs` and `shots`; `_numbers_read_as_a_score` also needs EACH number beside a team name on its own row (gap at most 6 digit heights, team not a stat word). Cost measured on all logs (1 night + today): 653 readings came from the text reader alone, 429 are now rejected (player lists, tables, ads: junk), 224 kept; replaying every tab with the old and the new code gives the same score events except the false (5, 3) is gone and one extra/shifted event on the already noisy UCLA-Oregon board (00:29). Not built: requiring a clock or quarter for a score change from 0-0 (34% of the real score frames have no readable clock on the same frame: 3893 of 5860).
* **GOAL banner, goal phrases, goal phrase watch (branch `scoring-bugs-42`, approved by the user 2026-10-11)**: (1) `GameTracker.goal_banner` (soccer only, text-only): the banner an operator flashes in the place of the scorebug's first team code and digits, for ONE reading, often before the score: whole items `GOAL`/`BUT`/`GOL`/`GOLO` (also `Goal!`, `GOOAL`), a broken pair (`G` + `AL`), or a lone `AL`/`OAL`/`GOA` when one of the two team codes read a moment ago is missing. Needs two team codes in sight (or one of the pair seen in the last 10 s), a live game, no stat word on screen, cooldown 30 s; never in `GOAL TO GO`, `FIELD GOAL`, `GOALS`, `Goal kick` (other whole items), nor in American football or unknown sports. Replayed on all logs of both soccer tabs (4419 frames): 5 hits, all real Man City goals (18:53:03, 18:56:43, 19:00:36, 19:03:40, 19:06:26; two are the user's rewinds), 0 on the 0-0 Como-Roma game. The server calls `scorer.set_event(tab, now, config.goal_banner_strength)` (0.4, the later start wins so a real score change takes it to full). (2) `speech.CUES['goal']` has the goal calls heard tonight (`prend les devants`, `ouvre la marque`, `double la mise`, `inscrit son 6e but`, `revient au score`, `met le deuxieme`, `porte le score a`, `what a goal`, `he scores`, `takes the lead`, `pulls one back`, `goool`, `golazo`...); 0 false hits on 1924 speech lines. (3) `cues.GoalPhraseWatch`: MEASURING ONLY, a goal phrase and a surge >= `goal_phrase_surge` 0.6 within `goal_phrase_window_s` 10 s on the same tab is logged as `goal phrase + surge would fire`; decide after a game day.
* **A new score during a replay counts at once (branch `scoring-bugs-43`, the user's rule 2026-10-11)**: before, `ScoreWatcher.update(..., replay=True)` returned at once (no reading at all) and a replay's clock jump threw the baseline away. Now, with a score already voted, a replay reading goes through the normal reading/vote/event path ONLY when `_is_news`: neither side lower than the ceiling (`_peak`) and at least one side higher by 1..`MAX_RISE`, in the scorebug's order; anything else (the same score, an older one, the sides swapped) is dropped without touching votes, the peak or the slow `PEAK_MEMORY_S` timer; a replay's clock does not reset the baseline. With no score known yet a replay reading is ignored. The event needs the usual 3 agreeing readings (about 9 s) and does not wait for the replay to end; the replay of the counted score gives no second event. Checked on tonight's logs: `tracker.replay` was never true on the Man City or Jaguars tabs (CANAL+ and bein show no REPLAY banner), so the gate was not what delayed them: the 0-2 at 19:03:48 and 0-3 at 19:06:42 were read 8 and 16 s after the GOAL banner (votes), the 0-2 was a rewind of a goal already counted, and the Jaguars touchdown (10 -> 16, 18:51:41) was never read because the separator after the code was read as `E` (`JAXE 16`; `E` added to `CODE_SCORE_RE`) and the scorebug is on screen only about 5 s.
* **A baseline poisoned by the NFL play clock and swallowed by a clock jump (branch `scoring-bugs-44`, approved by the user 2026-10-11; found with the last field goal, JAX 17-16 -> 17-19 at 19:14:17)**: (A) `find_score_geo` drops a number from the score candidates when it sits right of a panel item (clock, quarter/down ordinal `Ath`/`3RD`, `Q4`) on the same row within 4 text heights and is at most `PLAY_CLOCK_VS_PANEL` 1.3x as tall as it (the play clock is printed in the clock's size; checked on all logs: bein NFL bug 0.90-1.05, centre-panel college bugs 1.38-2.67, the Como-Roma junk 1.16-1.22). Known gap: when the panel is hidden under the drive card nothing can be measured against (one such frame cannot make a baseline). Removed the false (14, 25) score change of 18:56:13 and the junk baselines (16, 21). (B1) a clock jump to `QUARTER_END_S` 10 s or less of an American football quarter does not reset the baseline. (B2) after any reset the ceiling known before it (`_before_reset`: the higher of peak and voted score, when, and the game clock then) is kept `PRE_RESET_MEMORY_S` 300 s: the first new baseline is an EVENT when exactly ONE side rose by 1..`MAX_RISE`, the other did not move, and the game clock read now continues the one before the reset (`_same_game_clock`: moved by at most twice the elapsed time + 40 s); everything else (lower, equal, both sides different = another game in the slot) is silent. The clock guard removed a false 0-2 (Como-Roma at 90' replaced by an Espanyol-Elche tile at 28'). Whole-sequence test: `daemon/tests/fixtures/nfl_final_field_goal_1914.json` (real OCR boxes 19:09:30-19:14:40, tab 485660207) must give the PHI 17-16 field goal and the JAX 17-19 field goal, both strength 0.6.
* **Screenshots of 2026-10-11** (`daemon/tests/test_samples_1011.py`, boxes only): a pair token (`4-0`) is a score only when the team names are on its own row (`RECORD_ROW`; records sit below the names); `FRA 0-0` / `FRA 0-0 BEL` in one item; stacked scoreboards (ATM 2 / RMA 1, each digit beside its team; a clock+team item like `24.47 RMA` is split); a clock with two team codes (or one code merged with the clock, or `FRA 0-0`) is NOT an ad countdown (`has_scoreboard_text`: 3 of 8 screenshots wrongly read `break`). Reader limits found: the big digits of compact scorebugs are missed on the full frame but read on a 2x zoom of the area around the clock (proposed to the main session as a second pass in `screen_ocr.py`), the colon is read as a digit (`12853`, `09331`, `14848`), a white digit on a light plate is lost even zoomed (MCI 1-0 SUN).

* **Multiplex tabs (branch `scoring-bugs-93`, user option C 2026-10-18)**: the popup flag `multiplex` (register message, per tab) marks a tab whose one video jumps between games of the same sport. `game_identity.team_pair(texts)` = the two team codes of a reading (exactly two different codes; a ticker, a studio shot or one code gives none); `GameIdentity` (per tile, tracked on every tab) needs `scoring.new_game_readings` (3) equal readings in a row to set the game, and the same count of a DIFFERENT pair to report a new game (no pair = nothing changes). Only on a flagged tab `Server._on_new_game` resets that tile: `ScoreWatcher.new_game` (score, ceiling, announced goal, touchdown memory, clock), `GameTracker.new_game` (FINAL / HALF TIME and break memory, frozen clock, periods, down, clock filter: the new game's clock is accepted at once; the ad state stays), `Scorer.reset_tile` (event, goal hold, hot episode, cue), `event_at`, `held_change`, `unconfirmed`, `fed`, `replay_signal`, `fav_seen`, `rule_seen`, build-up and commentary memory of the tile, the ESPN lock. A flagged tab also ignores the popup teams / page text for favourites, slot rules and the feed mapping (only teams read on screen count). Logs `new game on tab N: AAA-BBB -> CCC-DDD` (events.log) and a `new_game` dataset event; dataset tile rows carry `game_key`. Unflagged tabs behave as before (tests/test_multiplex.py). Known limit: scorebugs with full names and no codes give no identity; the watcher can still fire from the new game's score on the first readings if its vote completes before the three code readings.
* **Goal jingle (branch `scoring-bugs-94`, user decision 2026-10-18: a strong cue directly, tweak later)**: CANAL+'s multiplex plays a short repeated-note sting when a goal is scored in ANY of its games, before the commentators say who. `daemon/jingle.py` (numpy only, CPU, about 8 ms per tab per comparison): the reference is cut to its notes and turned into a log-mel template offline (`scripts/build_jingle_template.py PATH`, writes git-ignored `models/jingle/jingle.npz`; the audio and template are never committed; no file = feature off, daemon unchanged). Live, `Server.handle_audio` keeps each tab's last (template + 1) s of audio; every `scoring.jingle_interval_s` (0.4) `JingleMatcher.best` slides the template over it (each mel band relative to its own median, zero-mean unit-norm correlation, 0 to 1). `JingleWatch` needs `jingle_min_score` (0.40) on `jingle_votes` (2) windows in a row that agree within 0.3 s on where the sting ended, then `jingle_cooldown_s` (8) of audio time. Measured on the real reference mixed into synthetic speech / crowd noise: 0.55 at 0 dB, 0.38 at -5 dB; speech, crowd, other music, an arpeggio and silence stay under 0.2. A hit logs `jingle heard tab N (score X)`, writes the dataset event `jingle` and, with `jingle_cue` 1, calls `Scorer.set_anticipation(weight=weights.jingle_cue_weight (0.50 = cue_review_top), strong=True)` on the tab's audible video (any sport: counts in full without another sign, like a football `is_strong_cue`) and notes `jingle` in the build-up tracker. It is a tab-level cue (the goal may be in another game of the channel); on a `multiplex` tab `_on_new_game` re-applies it (same start time) if it is still within `anticipation_s`. Unverified: the live false-hit rate (only synthetic noise tested) and the real timing against the goal; check by counting `jingle heard` lines against score changes on the CANAL+ tab.

*2. Constants and why*
* `SCORE_VS_LABEL` = 2.0: score digits must be 2x taller than the team names beside them. Records read as numbers reach 1.83x ("59", 66/36 px), real digits start at 2.07x (Big Ten, 112/54) and are usually 2.6-4.7x: the gap is narrow, change only with real numbers from `logs/boxes.log`.
* `PEAK_HOLD_S` 25 (a false high reading must not hide the next real goal), `PEAK_MEMORY_S` 120 (a lower value is believed only after this long), `FIRED_MEMORY_S` 60, `HISTORY`/`NEEDED` 5/3, `MAX_RISE` 10 (a 1-10 point jump is a score).
* `audio_ad_margin` 0.15 / votes 3 of 5 / `audio_ad_memory_s` 12: stadium music under commentary (music 0.7, speech 0.8) was labelled AD 76% of the time before the margin; real ads have music >> speech.
* `recent_ad_s` 30 (45 could keep MAIN on an ad), `confirm_s` 2, `leverage_weight` 0.3, `lev_*` close/blowout: American football 8/24 points, soccer 1/3 goals. `IN_PLAY_MEMORY_S` 15, `DOWN_MEMORY_S` 20, `OVERTIME_MEMORY_S` 30. All numbers are in `Config`, `settings.toml` and the settings LAYOUT (a test keeps them equal), except the `score_watch.py`/`game_state.py` module constants above.
* **Spoken touchdown pair rule (`scoring-bugs-98`, user decision 2026-10-18, option B, American football only).** A spoken `touchdown` stays the `high` cue (0.35, capped at `cue_alone_cap` when alone) but COUNTS AS A STRONG CUE (full, like a penalty awarded; starts a hot episode) when a loudness surge of at least `touchdown_pair_surge` (0.30, the normal surge signal) is seen on that stream within `touchdown_pair_window_s` (6 s) BEFORE or AFTER the call: `Scorer.set_touchdown_cue` checks `surge_hit_at` (kept by `add_audio`), and a surge arriving later upgrades the pending cue (`_Tile.pair_at`) if it is still the stored cue. It lasts the normal `anticipation_s`. `touchdown_pair_window_s` = 0 turns the rule off. Why: live miss Houston-Jacksonville 17:51:49 (cue 0.35, surge 0.70 at 17:51:56, peak 0.32 < min_score). Other cues and sports are unchanged. (`director_training/features.py` does not model the pair rule yet.)

* **Rewind ignores the score change** (`scoring.rewind_ignores_score`, default 1; found live 2026-10-18 18:31, CANAL+ Premier League tile, the user was scrubbing the stream): while the feed gate says a tile is rewinding (stream clock far behind its high point, see `feed_gate._watch_rewind`), a score change on it is a replayed goal. `Server._rewind_ignores` skips `set_event`/`event_at` (so no goal hold and no swap bonus), logs the feed line and records a dataset `score_change` with strength 0, verdict/note `rewind`. The "counted late" held change (a change read in a break) is dropped with a log line too. Safety: if the mapped feed shows the OCR score at the stream clock (`FeedMapper.allowed_scores`), the rewind flag is stale (live case 18:33 PAR-REN: the high point had been inflated to 84:10 by scrubbing) and the change counts; `FeedGate.distrust_rewind` ends the flag and restarts the high point at the current clock. A tile with no feed mapping has verdict 'unknown' and is unchanged. 0 = old behaviour. Not covered: a rewind that the gate never noticed (tile clock unread).
